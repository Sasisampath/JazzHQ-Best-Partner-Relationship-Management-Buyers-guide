# Best PRM Software — editorial review

Research checked November 10, 2026. The four-page JazzHQ PDF supplies the scope, platform order, evaluation questions, and updated positioning. The approved Euler Alternatives branch was a design and research reference, not evidence that all prior claims are current. Vendor documentation supports descriptions of advertised features; these are not independently tested. Best-for labels and tradeoffs are editorial judgments. JazzHQ publishes the guide and is included in the comparison. The guide has no numerical rankings, review aggregates, invented bylines, or asserted customer outcomes.
